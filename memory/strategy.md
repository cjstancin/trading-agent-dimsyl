# Strategy — Bull (aggressive paper) · APPROVED

> Approved by CJ 2026-06-12. This is the active aggressive playbook for the paper sandbox. Refine it through the weekly review (propose changes, don't apply silently).

## Objective
Beat the S&P 500 by a wide margin in paper by taking concentrated, high-conviction swing & momentum trades. Embrace variance. Learn what blows up.

## What to BUY (entry triggers)
Take a position when **2+** of these align (more = bigger conviction):
1. **Breakout:** price clears a multi-week/52-week high on above-average volume.
2. **Momentum leader:** strongest names in a hot sector (semis, AI, crypto-proxies) showing relative strength vs SPY.
3. **Oversold snapback:** quality/high-beta name down 15%+ on no broken fundamentals, turning up off support.
4. **Catalyst:** near-term, dated event (earnings beat/guide-up, product, FDA, crypto catalyst) from P1's approved-cycle or your own scan.
5. **Leverage the thesis:** when conviction in a sector is high and regime is RISK-ON, express it with a leveraged ETF (TQQQ/SOXL) sized within the 40% cap.

Prefer ideas already scored in `../Finance-Research/Signals/approved-cycle.md`; use own scans for fast intraday setups it missed.

## What to SELL (exits)
- **Stop:** ~18% trailing on every entry (wider for leveraged/crypto). Never remove a stop.
- **Cut:** down ~−12% from entry → exit unless a dated catalyst is imminent and thesis intact.
- **Target/scale:** trim into strength at the first target; let a runner ride with a tightened trailing stop.
- **Thesis break:** the reason you bought is gone → exit regardless of P&L.
- **Time stop:** dead money after ~2–3 weeks with no progress → recycle the capital.

## Sizing (run before every entry)
`shares = (0.10 × equity) ÷ (entry − stop)` → cap at **40%** position → check sector ≤80%, ≤6 open, ≤6 new/week, ~10% cash buffer. Show shares, cost, resulting %.

## Regime overlay
- **RISK-ON** (VIX <16): full aggression; leveraged/crypto allowed.
- **NEUTRAL** (16–24): trim new sizes ~25%; be choosier.
- **RISK-OFF** (>24 or major macro event): high-conviction only; avoid fresh leveraged/crypto; consider standing down.
- Honor the **−10% daily halt** and **−30% monthly kill** always.

## Crypto notes
Trade only liquid majors (BTC, ETH, and top names Alpaca supports). 24/7 market — manage on each weekday run; size within the same caps; expect bigger gaps.

## What NOT to do
No sub-$2 junk, no zero-volume names, no naked (stopless) positions, no margin, no options (paper config), no widening limits mid-trade.
